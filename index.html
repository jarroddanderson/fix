Portrait or editorial — what’s the difference?

It isn’t who had the idea. It’s whether the picture has to be built.

A portrait

A standard setup. One lighting design, two looks from your own wardrobe, and my direction on the day. Bring references for mood or light and I’ll shoot toward them — that’s included, not extra.

An editorial

A built setup. Sourced props, styling, a scouted location, more than one environment. That work happens days before the shoot — which is what you’re paying for. Scouting and concepting are in the fee; any venue or prop spend is passed through at cost, agreed up front.

Already have a moodboard? Bring it — either tier. The price follows what the shoot needs built, not who thought of it. And whichever way we go, I’ll tell you honestly what will work and what won’t before we shoot it.

Good to Know

FAQ

How do I receive my images?+
Your edited gallery is delivered via a private online gallery link within 7–10 business days of your session. Rush delivery is available as an add-on — 3-day (+$300), 2-day (+$500), or next day (+$750).
What's the deposit to book?+
A 50% deposit is required to secure your date. The remaining balance is due on the day of the session. Deposits are non-refundable but transferable if you reschedule with at least 48 hours notice.
What should I wear?+
Wear what feels like you — elevated. Solid colors and simple patterns typically photograph best. Avoid logos unless they're intentional. If you're unsure, I'm happy to advise during our pre-shoot consultation. Wardrobe styling is available as an add-on ($600), as is hair & makeup ($350–450 for sessions).
Do you travel for shoots?+
Yes. I'm based in Atlanta, Los Angeles, and New York — and available worldwide. Travel outside these markets may include a travel fee (flights, hotel, ground transport) discussed during consultation.
Do you take on commercial and brand work?+
Yes — that's most of what I do. Day rates start at $1,800 (half day) and $3,200 (full day), plus usage. If a project needs full production — casting, styling, crew, locations, post — that runs through Purple Spider Agency, starting at $4,500.
Can you shoot product without us being there?+
Yes. Ship your product to the Atlanta studio and we handle concept, styling, shooting and post — you approve remotely. It's how a lot of our ongoing brand work runs.
How does licensing work?+
All sessions include personal use rights. If your images will be used commercially — ads, billboards, product campaigns, editorial publishing — a licensing fee applies depending on scope, duration, and distribution. We discuss and agree before delivery. No surprises.
Does the fee cover the location itself?+
The work of finding and securing a location is included — that's part of what an editorial fee buys. The location's own cost isn't. Public spaces, your space or mine are covered at no charge. If we want a specific venue, studio or anything needing a permit, that's billed at cost with no markup, and I'll always get your sign-off on the number before anything is booked. Same applies to props and wardrobe we have to buy or rent.
I have my own moodboard — can you just shoot it?+
Yes, and it's welcome. Which tier it lands in depends on what the images need built, not who put the board together. References for mood, colour or lighting fit inside a standard portrait session. If it calls for sourced props, styling, a scouted location or several set-ups, that's an editorial. Either way we'll talk it through first — I'll tell you what will work, what won't, and where I'd push it further.
Can I request specific shots or moods?+
Absolutely. Every booking includes a pre-shoot consultation where we align on creative direction, references, and shot list. Come with a vision or let me bring one — either works.
What's your cancellation policy?+
Cancellations within 48 hours of the session forfeit the deposit. Reschedules with 48+ hours notice are accommodated once at no charge. Same-day cancellations are billed at 50% of the full session rate.
Do you shoot tethered?+
Yes, tethered shooting is available for studio sessions — great for clients who want to see images in real time and provide on-set direction. Let me know when booking.
